## Overview

Medio downloads video and audio from the platforms supported by yt-dlp and converts them with FFmpeg, in a desktop application built around a queue. You choose the streams, format and quality; Medio plans the download and conversion, keeps both tools current by itself, and can repeat a workflow through saved profiles, schedules and watch folders.

> Download only media you have the right to use. Medio does not circumvent DRM or other access protection, and source availability depends on each platform.

### Download

- Video in MP4, MKV, AVI, WEBM, WMV, MOV and FLV, up to 1440p in Basic and up to 4K and 8K in Premium
- Audio in MP3, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WAV, M4A, AAC and WMA, with format-aware quality presets
- Playlists and collections, a download queue, and Clip & Trim for a single time range
- SponsorBlock segment marking or removal, subtitles, thumbnails and embedded metadata

### Convert

- Hardware-accelerated conversion on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics
- H.264 in Basic; H.265, VP8, VP9, AV1 and MPEG-4 with two-pass encoding in Premium
- Audio normalisation in five modes, and in Premium pitch and speed, enhancement, remastering and creative effects

### Automate

- Saved download profiles, schedules, subscriptions and watch folders
- Intake rules and rule packages in Premium
- Settings, profiles and download history synchronised across your computers when you sign in

## Installation and updates

1. Download the installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/Arctisoft-Studio/Medio-Universal-Downloader/releases/latest).
2. Run it and follow the setup. It installs for your Windows user account, so no administrator rights are needed. Setup can also install Studio Hub alongside it.
3. Medio checks for new versions on the update channel you choose: Stable, Preview or Manual.

Updates are taken only from this repository's official releases. Before an update runs, its installer is checked against the size and SHA-256 digest that GitHub records for the release, and an installation signed by Arctisoft Studio accepts only an installer from the same publisher. Medio can always update by itself; [Studio Hub](https://github.com/Arctisoft-Studio/Arctisoft-Studio-Hub) offers the same releases from one place.

## Account and privacy
